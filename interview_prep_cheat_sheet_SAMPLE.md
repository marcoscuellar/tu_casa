## THE SHEET

> *Sample intel — your live sheet pulls the real, current research for Northwind.*

`Marketing`

# Prepping for Content Strategist at Northwind.

Meeting **Dana Lee** · VP of Marketing

---

### 01 · Why Marketing is hiring

- Marketing at Northwind is investing — this opening most likely tracks a new growth or repositioning push.
- They'll want to see how you move pipeline, CAC, and brand lift in the first 90 days.

---

### 02 · Recent Marketing news

- Watch for recent Marketing announcements from Northwind — bring one up to show you did the homework. — *live research · updated at generation*
- Check Northwind's newsroom and the team's LinkedIn for what shipped this quarter. — *live research*

---

### 03 · Org & leadership changes

- Look for a recent Marketing leadership hire or re-org at Northwind — new leaders bring new priorities.
- Know where Dana Lee sits in the org and who they report to.

---

### 04 · What they're launching

- Northwind is likely working toward a rebrand or a new demand-gen motion — tie your experience to it.
- Have one story ready that maps directly to what Marketing is shipping next.

---

### 05 · What Dana Lee cares about

- As a leader, Dana Lee will probe for judgment and outcomes over task lists.
- Be ready to speak to pipeline and brand impact, not activity.
- Be ready to speak to how you measure what works.
- Be ready to speak to clear storytelling.

---

### 06 · Posture

You're not being judged — you're both figuring out if this fits. Slow down, answer the question they asked, and it's fine to take two seconds before you speak.

---

- ☐ Two wins ready to tell as stories

If live research comes back **THIN**, §02–§04 shrink or say "couldn't confirm" rather than filling with plausible text. The sheet still works; it just leans on §01, §05, §06 and §07.

## VARIANT — no title, no name

Same company and role, intake left with only `Content Strategist`:

- `Content Strategist` matches `content` → department is still **Marketing**.
- Interviewer is unknown → `who` falls back to **"your interviewer"**; §05's heading becomes *What your interviewer cares about*.
- Not leadership → §05 opens **"your interviewer will likely go deep on the day-to-day craft of the Content Strategist role."**
- §03's second bullet reads "Know where your interviewer sits in the org and who they report to."

And with a title the rules don't recognize at all — say *Chief of Staff* with no role given — the department resolves to **"the team"**, §01 reads *Why the team is hiring*, and the flavor falls back to concrete-examples/ownership. Thinner, still honest, still usable.
